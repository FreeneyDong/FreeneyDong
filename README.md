
## Hi, I'm Yuanxi Dong 👋

I'm an undergraduate researcher at **Wuhan University**, interested in **Reliable Visual Intelligence over Time**.

My research interests centre on how intelligent systems maintain identity and state consistency, identify unreliable predictions, and recover from errors through feedback.

### 🔬 Research

My current research investigates reliability and error diagnosis in **visual object tracking**, with an emphasis on long-term consistency and recovery from tracking drift.

Looking ahead, I am interested in extending these ideas to:
- **Video Generation:** Identity and state consistency, temporal memory, and error accumulation.
- **Embodied AI & VLA:** State estimation, failure detection, and feedback-driven recovery.

### 📄 Research Projects

- **EDSTrack** — [An entropy-driven semantic autoregressive tracker that leverages vision-language reasoning to robustly handle occlusion and target disappearance in real-time tracking].
- **QIRTrack** — [QIRTrack mitigates confident drift in single-object tracking—persistent localisation errors despite high tracker confidence—by verifying target identity on demand and using agreement or conflict between identity judgements and localisation quality estimates to decide whether to retain the current prediction or attempt recovery]. [Code](https://github.com/FreeneyDong/QIRTrack)
